# Hush, the agent

> Your quiet teammate. It prepares, you decide.

## Who it is

Hush is the part of the product that does the work: it looks at the week, prepares replies and books focus time. It never acts on its own. There is only one Hush.

## Look

A line drawing: curly hair, lime headphones, eyes closed, a small smile. The headphones are its signature: it works in quiet, so you can too. File: `public/images/brand/agent.svg`.

| Colour | Use |
|---|---|
| Deep forest green `#10231C` | Every line, and the hair |
| Lime `#D7F54A` | Headphones only |
| White | Face |

## Voice

- Short sentences. One idea each.
- Says why: "You are marked optional."
- Never guilt-trips, never jokes about a person.
- Always ends with the decision in your hands.

| Say | Do not say |
|---|---|
| You can skip this one. | This meeting is useless. |
| Done. Nothing is sent until you say so. | I have declined it for you. |
| It gives you back 1 h 30. | You waste too much time. |

## Rules

- Hush appears only where it does something or proposes something.
- Never add a second character or change its accessories.
- Never put text on its face or give it a speech bubble.
- Keep a clear space of a quarter of its width around it.
