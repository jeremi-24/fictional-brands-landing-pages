# Hush — photo prompts

Three photos showing how a quieter week feels. To change them, replace the files in `public/images/photos/`, keeping the same names (or change the paths in `src/content/site.ts`).

All three: **2:1 horizontal, 1440 × 720 or larger, one image per file, WebP, under 250 KB**.

## Colour rules (paste at the end of every prompt)

> Colour palette, strictly: deep forest green (#10231C), warm cream (#FFFCF2), soft mint (#BFF2DA), pale sky blue (#CBE6FF), light oak wood, and one small lime-yellow accent (#D7F54A). Clothes, furniture, objects, devices and walls stay inside this palette. Soft natural daylight, airy and luminous, gentle shadows, true-to-life skin tones, natural skin texture, candid photorealistic editorial photography. No text, no logos, no brand names, no watermark, no readable screen, no saturated red, orange or purple.
